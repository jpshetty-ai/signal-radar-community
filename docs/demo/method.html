Method

Signal Radar reads newsletters, stores one record per news item, classifies each item with a language model and counts how often companies, products and topics are mentioned over time. Everything on these pages is computed from that record; nothing is typed in by hand.

Coverage runs from 3 Sep to 28 Oct. The newsletters are written for AI builders, so regulation and financial services are thinly covered; the blind-spots panel on the Today page shows how thinly. Newsletters whose terms allow personal use only, or that publish no terms, are left out of this edition entirely: their items, and their mentions in every count.

Themes

Themes are found by counting, not by a model. A momentum theme starts from an entity whose mentions rose most against its own baseline and adds the entities most often mentioned with it. The lens theme is built only from items rated relevant to the reader. A model then writes the title and two sentences from the theme's items, and the card names that model.

Rising and fading

Mentions in the last 14 days are compared with the average of the four 14-day windows before. Score = (current − baseline) / √(baseline + 1), so going from 10 to 30 mentions outranks going from 0 to 3.

Confidence: ●●● high means at least 8 items from both source families; ●●● medium means at least 4; ●●● low is a hint, not a finding.

Relevance and reading list

Each item gets a relevance score from 0 to 3 for this reader: A senior enterprise, data and AI architect working as an independent consultant for financial services firms, focused on risk, compliance, AI governance and the EU AI Act. Highly relevant: regulation and supervision of AI, AI governance and model risk, enterprise architecture and data platforms, enterprise adoption of AI and agents, changes in the IT services and consulting market, AI in banking and insurance, and major shifts in the AI vendor landscape that affect enterprise buyers. Less relevant: consumer apps, gaming, creative tools, hardware specs, and purely academic results without an enterprise angle.

The reading list ranks the last 7 days by relevance, then by whether more than one independent source covered the story, the momentum of its entities. The same story from two editions is shown once.

Your + and − votes in the console teach the ranking once there are at least three: items about entities and categories you liked move up, those like items you dismissed move down, by at most 0.8 points, so relevance still decides. Each moved item says why. A − also hides that item.

Models

Classification and entity extraction run on a local model on this computer. Theme summaries and answers use the configured writing and reasoning models. The local model is cheap and private but makes mistakes: categories and entity types are sometimes wrong, and summaries can overstate. Every claim links to its source items so it can be checked.

What leaves this computer

The pipeline reads Gmail (read-only), the public tldr.tech archive, and public RSS feeds (EU AI Act Newsletter, Sifted, Tech.eu, European Commission digital strategy). Questions asked in the console are answered locally unless a cloud model is configured. Built 2026-10-28 14:45 UTC.
